ANCHOR

nine truths for the authentic builder

by Jessica Chavez aka Tica Rey

Feed your soul. Be the best you.

Entry One

Clear Intentions

Before you build, decide what is steering you.

Most people spend their lives chasing goals. Goals are useful. But goals are destinations. Intentions are direction.

A destination can change. Direction should not.

Why are you building this? Why does it matter? Who are you becoming while you build it?

Those questions matter more than timelines and five year plans. Because intentions survive what goals cannot. They survive setbacks. They survive detours. They survive success.

Without intention, every opportunity looks important. Every distraction looks urgent. Every opinion sounds like wisdom.

With intention, decisions become simpler. You know what belongs. You know what doesn't.

And inside that intention there must be patience. Not passive waiting. Active patience. The kind that continues the work without demanding immediate results. The kind that understands a strong foundation is invisible before it is impressive.

Be intentional with your time. Be intentional with your energy. Be intentional with your relationships.

Build with purpose, not pressure.

A destination can change. Direction should not.

Entry Two

What This Is

This book is not a map. It is a mirror.

You picked it up because something in you already knows you are capable of more than one thing. You already feel it. And somewhere else inside you, people have told you that's impossible. Too ambitious. Too much.

This book exists to tell you the feeling is real. You are capable of more than people think. More than you know.

You are not spreading yourself too thin. Find your balance as You are expanding into who you actually are.

Not everyone will understand your vision. That is okay. Have compassion for them. Send back good energy. It is simply the nature of the territory you are walking into.

Don't take it personal. Spend time on thoughts on things that feed you. You are carrying something heavier than most. A vision that spans multiple worlds, multiple expressions of yourself. That comes with its own weight. Its own crown.

Stay focused. Stay compassionate. Keep going.

This book is not a map. It is a mirror.

Entry Three

Foundation

You cannot build a meaningful life on borrowed values.

Before you build anything, know who you are. Not your resume. Not your goals. Not the version of yourself you perform for other people.

Who are you when nobody is watching?

What moves you? What do you believe in when there is nothing to prove? What makes you feel alive even when everything around you is chaos?

Have you given yourself time to answer that?

Because you can get everything you want and still lose yourself in it. People reach the goal. The money, the recognition, the thing they always said they wanted. And they stand there empty because they built toward something external instead of building from something internal.

That is the trap.

If your foundation is shaky, if you are chasing someone else's definition of success, someone else's version of what you should be, then when you get there you won't recognize yourself.

But if you know who you are? If you have sat with yourself long enough to understand your own heart, your own values, your own truth? Nothing can shake you loose. No success. No failure. No opinion.

That becomes your anchor.

You cannot build a meaningful life on borrowed values.

Entry Four

Your Talents

Honesty about your starting point is the beginning of mastery.

Knowing who you are is step one. Knowing what you are actually good at is step two.

And here is where most people spend time on.

If you want to be good at something so badly you could save time and energy by building and learning the foundations, practice and just be, live it.

Be truly honest with yourself. What are you actually talented at right now? Not what you wish you were good at. Not what sounds impressive. What flows? what comes naturally? What do people come to you for?

But hear this. If something doesn't come naturally, that doesn't mean you can't master it. Everyone starts from zero and become exceptional through relentless practice. Through showing up. Through being willing to be bad at it first and staying committed anyway.

The difference is they were honest about where they started. They knew it would take extra time, extra effort, extra repetition. And they did it anyway because the vision was worth it.

Find that intersection. Who you are, what you are genuinely skilled at or willing to master, what you actually want to build. Be real about the gap. Be real about the hard work required.

That is where your real strategy begins.

Honesty about your starting point is the beginning of mastery.

Entry Five

Ideas

Not every idea deserves your life.

You will get ideas constantly. Some will come from inspiration. Some from fear. Some from watching what other people are doing and thinking you should do it too.

But the ones that matter? The ones worth your time and energy? They are the ones that align with who you actually are and what you are naturally drawn to.

Pay attention to what comes naturally. What do you create without being taught? What lights you up without requiring external motivation? What feels like an extension of who you are instead of a role you are playing?

Those are your ideas.

But if an idea calls to you and the talent isn't there yet, that is not a no. That is a different kind of yes. A yes that will take extra time, extra practice, extra commitment. If you are willing to put in that work, go for it.

The difference is knowing the cost. Being honest about what you are signing up for. Not wasting yourself on ideas that don't actually matter to you.

Prioritize what comes naturally. But don't kill ideas that call to your soul just because the path is longer.

Not every idea deserves your life.

Entry Six

Time

Every yes is a no to something else.

Time is the one resource you cannot buy back. You can make more money. Find new opportunities. Start over. But you cannot make more time. Once it is gone it is gone.

So how you spend your time is how you spend your life.

Most people say yes to everything because they want to help, because they don't want to disappoint. And then they wonder why they never made progress on their real vision.

Here is the hard truth. You cannot say yes to everything. Even when you want to. Even when it would make other people happy.

Be intentional about what you say yes to. What aligns with your vision? What feeds your foundation? What actually moves you forward? Say yes to that. Say no to everything else.

Some people will take that no personally. They won't understand why your time isn't theirs to claim. Have compassion for that. They are operating from a place where they don't see what you see. But don't let their hurt stop you from protecting what is yours.

There is a time for everything. Trust that you are on time. Even when it doesn't feel like it.

Your vision requires it. Your sanity requires it. Your life requires it.

Every yes is a no to something else.

Entry Seven

The Anchor

An anchor doesn't stop movement. It stops drifting.

You are enough. Right now, exactly as you are, you are enough to start.

You know yourself. You believe in yourself. You have done the work to understand your foundation, your talents, your real vision. That is not nothing. That is everything.

So when the doubt comes, and it will come, remember this. You are not alone. There are other authentic souls doing exactly what you are doing. Grinding from love, not ego. Building with their whole heart. You are part of that. You are part of something real.

Keep going. Not because it is easy. Because it is yours. Some days that looks like massive progress. Some days it looks like showing up and doing one small thing.

Both count. Both matter.

Trust the process. Everything happening right now, the detours, the timing, the moments that don't feel productive, it is all building you into who you need to be to carry what you are building.

Allow yourself to heal. Healing is not separate from the work. It is part of becoming your best self. Tend to yourself with the same care you give your vision.

You are on time. You are enough. You are not alone.

The struggle will come. Let the art of struggle make you better.

An anchor doesn't stop movement. It stops drifting.

Entry Eight

Patience Is a Superpower

Patience is movement without panic.

Let's be clear about what patience is not. It is not waiting. It is not stalling. It is not hiding behind trusting the process when what you actually need to do is move.

That is fear wearing spiritual clothing.

Real patience is active. It is showing up every single day and doing the work while trusting the timing will align. It is moving with intention while releasing the need to control the outcome.

Patience means you don't rush the wrong things. You don't force doors that aren't ready to open. You don't sacrifice the quality of what you are building just to say you finished fast.

But patience also means you don't use the process as a crutch. You don't keep preparing forever. You don't stay comfortable in the becoming and never actually arrive.

Know the difference. Are you being patient or are you procrastinating? Are you trusting the process or are you afraid to launch?

Be honest with yourself.

Patience keeps you grounded when everyone around you is reactive. It keeps you focused when distractions are everywhere. It keeps you sane when the timeline doesn't look the way you expected.

Use it wisely. Use it with intention. But never use it as an excuse to stop moving.

Patience is movement without panic.

Entry Nine

Beyond What Seems Possible

Your limits are often inherited before they are tested.

You are capable of more than you have been told. More than you have been shown. More than you currently believe.

Most people operate inside the limits handed to them. The limits of their environment, their upbringing, what they have seen done before. And because they have never seen it, they don't believe it is possible. So they never try.

But you are reading this because something in you refuses to accept those limits.

Push past what seems humanly possible. Not recklessly, but intentionally. With your foundation solid, your talents sharp, your vision clear. Push past the version of yourself that plays it safe. Push past the doubt that says you are doing too much. Push past the exhaustion that tells you to quit right before the breakthrough.

The things worth building have never been done exactly the way you are going to do them. Your combination of talents, vision, experience, and heart is singular. Nobody has ever been exactly you, building exactly this, in exactly this moment.

That is not pressure. That is power.

So go further. Dream bigger. Build bolder. Become someone even you didn't know was possible.

You were never meant to fit inside someone else's idea of what's possible.

Your limits are often inherited before they are tested.
